# Data license and attribution

The transmission topology in this case is a derived database built from
OpenStreetMap data downloaded through the Overpass API. © OpenStreetMap
contributors. OpenStreetMap data are available under the Open Database License
(ODbL) 1.0: <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

Maryland electric utility service-territory polygons were downloaded from the
Maryland public ArcGIS service identified in `references/source_data_register.csv`.
Users who redistribute the case should retain that source attribution and check
the source portal's current terms.

Generator and chronology inputs are derived from existing HOPE ModelCases and
the PJM DC Health study inputs. Their lineage is recorded in
`references/source_data_register.csv`.

The inferred electrical parameters, generator-to-bus mappings, curation rules,
and external-corridor proxy are modeling assumptions, not authoritative utility
or PJM data. They are documented in the case README and reference tables.
